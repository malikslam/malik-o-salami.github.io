Research

Four questions about the scholarly record

Each pillar attacks the same underlying problem — accurate, trustworthy linkage between records and reality — from a different point in the pipeline.

Live demo — one retraction, four sources

Every source above corresponds to real disagreement patterns found across large-scale cross-database retraction indexing analysis — this is the exact class of problem my research measures.
01 Retraction & Metadata Indexing Quality ▾
Auditing whether retracted publications are consistently and accurately indexed across major bibliographic databases — Crossref, Retraction Watch, Scopus, and Web of Science — and diagnosing where the scholarly record disagrees with itself.
QSS (accepted) ASIS&T MET-STI 2024 — Best Paper STI 2023
02 Citation Data Infrastructure ▾
Building large-scale, reliability-filtered citation datasets that other researchers can trust and reuse — the data-curation counterpart to auditing indexing quality.
uCite — Data in Brief, 2025
03 Scholarly Network Modeling ▾
Using probabilistic machine learning over 130M+ PubMed and ProQuest records to infer mentor–mentee relationships — modeling the institutional structures that shape how research gets done in the first place.
Student Researcher, 2024–present HPC / Slurm
04 Open Science Infrastructure & Industry Translation ▾
Turning quality findings into open pipelines, datasets, and direct feedback for the organizations that maintain the scholarly record — funded in part by the Sloan Foundation.
Crossref Clarivate Dimensions $250K Sloan Foundation methodology
